Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the way people work, learn, and solve problems. It enables computers to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing images, and making decisions. Machine Learning, a subset of AI, allows systems to improve their performance by learning from data without being explicitly programmed. AI is widely used in healthcare, education, finance, transportation, and customer service. As technology continues to evolve, AI is expected to play an even greater role in improving efficiency, automating repetitive tasks, and supporting innovation across industries.
